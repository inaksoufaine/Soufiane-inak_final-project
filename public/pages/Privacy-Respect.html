GUIDING PRINCIPLES

How to handle sensitive stories with care and integrity.

✏️

Active
Listening

Prioritize the voice of the survivor. Allow them to share their experiences in their own words, ensuring their perspective remains at the heart of the story.

🔒

Preserve
Anonymity

Protect vulnerable sources by changing names, blurring identifying details, and ensuring no metadata compromises their safety.

🤝

Trauma-Informed
Focus

Approach every interview with empathy. Avoid sensationalizing or re-traumatizing survivors, and provide resources for support at the end.
